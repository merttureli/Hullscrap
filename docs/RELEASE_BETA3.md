# Hullscrap Beta 3

Everything from the second playtest, plus the owner's later notes. Download it from **GitHub → Actions → beta builds → the latest green run → Artifacts** (Windows, Linux, macOS). The main menu's corner says **Beta 3**.

## Starting out
- **You start alone**: the soldier and a **repair drone**. The drone repairs, fights fires and reloads ammo racks by itself, and uses 1 power with no cable. It never dies: when it breaks, hold E next to it to fix it. More drones come from the Meridian store or a salvage scan.
- **Ada** waits in an escape pod in stages 1–2 (about 7 runs in 10). After her, **crew are rare**: one free offer per region at most, 450 credits to hire, and only some stations have anyone.
- **Lean starters**: a shield or a medbay, not both; 1–2 guns; 250 scrap.
- **A huge round build area**: every starter sits in a circle 77 cells across.

## Building
- **Tier 1–2 parts are half the size inside** (2×2 guns and engines, 2×4 reactors and shields). Tier 3+ stay big. The medbay is one bed.
- **Angled mounts**: guns and engines snap onto 45° and 1:2 sloped hull faces at their true angle. An angled engine pushes forward and sideways at once; an angled turret's arc follows the slope.
- **The build menu shows only what you hold or can craft.** Everything else is in the crafting menu.
- **Every part explains itself**: coloured keywords (LASER, MISSILE, ION, AUTO, RAPID…), each with a one-line meaning on hover, compact stats and one line of lore.
- **The mouse wheel over a panel scrolls it** and no longer zooms.

## Flying
- **WASD follows the screen**, not the ship.
- **Every engine gives 30% of its thrust sideways**, so rear engines can strafe; side engines still do it best.
- **Open space has no walls.** New places keep appearing wherever you fly, and there are more events.
- **Debris flows steadily** at any speed, in 1–3 currents. Open-space cruise is slower (×1.8).
- **The Hollow pushes you**: as it nears, the debris thins, violet fog creeps in from its side, and the clock pulses red.

## Fighting
- **Shields come back slowly**: a Mk1 layer takes 7 s, one layer at a time, plus 3 s once a side was emptied.
- **Most guns fire slower and hit harder.** Only 6 rapid-fire guns remain; they are auto turrets (T toggles), so your hands are free for missiles.
- **Early enemies are lighter**, and few carry shields in stages 1–2.
- **Repairs take about twice as long.**

## Commanding the crew
- **The station bar** (left edge): select a crewmate, click a station, and they're posted there for good. They leave only for their own station's fire, repairs or ammo.
- **Breaches**: when a room starts losing air, time slows. R repairs; V evacuates and locks the room. Click its red tag to reopen it.
- **Hurt crew leave airless rooms** instead of repairing until they die. Suits protect against vacuum.

## Story
- **A beat in every stage**: the Snap, the gates, the Hollow, Halcyon. Ada's recorder carries her part of it, and wardens' hails echo the stage.
- **A guaranteed ending** at the Gate if you followed no other thread.
- **Story logs**: all 40 are found in play now. **J on the map opens the journal.**
- **Characters**:
  - every recruit has a backstory on their crew card, and the 50 unique characters show their bios;
  - starter ships have histories;
  - enemy captains hail you by name, with their own faction's lines.
- **Events**: 57 new ones, and the old ones were rewritten for a solo run.

## Saves
Continue keeps your crew (posts, injuries, the dead), the drone, angled mounts and the places you've finished. Older saves still load.
